# Document Control SOP

## 5.1 Document Identification

Controlled documents shall contain:
- document number
- title
- version number

## 5.2 Effective Date

Controlled documents shall display an effective date before release.

## 5.3 Approval

Controlled documents shall contain evidence of approval by the designated approver.

## 5.4 Review

Documents should be reviewed periodically according to the applicable review schedule.

Important: the "should" rule is intentionally included as a modality test.
